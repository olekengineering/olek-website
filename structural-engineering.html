Structural engineering support from concept to construction

Engineering requirements vary from project to project. Our services can be tailored to support property owners, contractors, developers and steel fabricators during planning, design and construction.

Structural Design

Structural analysis and design support for steel and reinforced concrete structures based on the requirements of the project.

Structural Drawings

Clear engineering drawings developed to support construction, coordination and communication between project teams.

Engineering support for warehouses, portal frames, industrial buildings, roof structures, extensions and other structural steel works.

Construction Support

Engineering coordination, technical clarification and site support during construction where required by the project.
